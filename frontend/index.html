07 FAQ

Before we
begin.

The essentials, answered simply. For anything specific to your date, talk to the team directly.

01How do I book Rakzs Studio?
Message us on WhatsApp or send an Instagram DM to @rakzsstudio.
02What do you shoot?
Weddings, pre-weddings, portraits, parties and corporate events — in photography, film, or combined coverage.
03Do you travel for shoots?
Tell us where the shoot is planned and ask the team on WhatsApp. We will discuss what is possible for your date and location.
04What do we receive?
A hand-edited photograph gallery and, when film coverage is part of your collection, your finished film. The exact deliverables are discussed before booking.
05How do we start planning?
Share your date, venue or city, and what you are celebrating. We will begin with a short consultation about the coverage you need.
06Can we book photography and film together?
Yes. Rakzs Studio covers both photography and film. Share your event details and the team will discuss the right combination with you.
